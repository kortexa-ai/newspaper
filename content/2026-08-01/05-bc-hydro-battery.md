On July 30, BC Hydro issued a request for supplier qualifications for
a battery energy storage facility near Duncan, on Vancouver Island —
the first utility-scale battery storage project in British Columbia's
history. The plan calls for at least 100 megawatts of storage capacity
sited next to the existing Vancouver Island Terminal Substation, able
to discharge stored power within seconds when demand on the island
spikes ([BC government](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026ECS0039-000848);
[ConstructConnect](https://canada.constructconnect.com/joc/news/projects/2026/07/rfq-to-be-issued-b-c-s-first-major-battery-storage-project-takes-a-step-forward)).

Batteries like this one don't generate electricity; they store it,
charging when supply is cheap and abundant and discharging when it
isn't, which lets a grid built for average demand handle its worst
peaks without building — and mostly idling — new peaker plants. BC
Hydro has identified up to 500 megawatts of similar battery storage
potential across the province, making the Duncan project a first
test case for a strategy it intends to repeat.

"B.C.'s first utility-scale battery storage facility will help power
the Island and is a key part of BC Hydro's Powering Growth plan," said
Adrian Dix, the province's Minister of Energy and Climate Solutions.
BC Hydro president and CEO Charlotte Mitha framed it as a matter of
keeping pace: "This first project will help ensure we continue
delivering reliable electricity to our customers as demand grows."
Cowichan Valley MLA Debra Toporowski noted the choice of location
plainly: "BC Hydro has selected the Cowichan Valley as the site for
its very first battery energy storage system."

The qualification process is the opening step, with a full request for
proposals expected later this year and the facility targeted to be
operational by 2030 — a reminder that grid infrastructure moves on a
slower clock than press releases do. But the shape of the plan is the
story here: rather than treating batteries as a novelty, BC Hydro is
using its first one to build the playbook for four or five more.
