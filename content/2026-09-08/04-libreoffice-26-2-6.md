The Document Foundation shipped LibreOffice 26.2.6 this week, the sixth
maintenance release for the 26.2 line, closing out 36 bugs across Writer,
Calc, Impress, and Base
([blog.documentfoundation.org](https://blog.documentfoundation.org/);
[Linuxiac](https://linuxiac.com/libreoffice-26-2-6-open-source-office-suite-released-with-36-bug-fixes/)).
Two fixes stand out for how ordinary the problems were: image brightness,
contrast, and transparency adjustments, broken in earlier 26.2 builds,
work again, and a bug that could leave a saved document permanently stuck
in read-only mode after a "Save As" is resolved.

The rest of the list reads like the small, unglamorous work that keeps a
free office suite trustworthy. Writer no longer crashes when footnote
settings are changed in HTML documents, and a bug that truncated title
text is fixed. In Calc, shared spreadsheets get a real data-loss fix:
drag-fill and autofill changes made by one collaborator now actually save
for everyone else, instead of silently vanishing. Impress users get back
working audio playback and fewer PPTX import crashes, and PDF exports
keep their hyperlinks intact. Windows, Linux/Plasma, and macOS users each
get their own round of platform-specific polish, from a Mail Merge dialog
that was unreadable in Windows' light theme to a macOS window-jitter bug.

None of it is a headline feature. All of it is the difference between an
office suite people tolerate and one they trust with a shared file.
