---

Moonshot AI, a Beijing-based lab, released Kimi K3 on July 16 as an
open-weight successor to its Kimi K2 line: a 2.8-trillion-parameter
mixture-of-experts model with a 1-million-token context window, tuned for
long coding sessions and multi-step agent work, with its full weights
published on Hugging Face for anyone to download and run.

On August 6, GitHub made it a first-class option inside Copilot, rolling
it out across every plan — Pro, Pro+, Max, Business, and Enterprise — and
every surface Copilot runs on, including VS Code, Visual Studio,
JetBrains, Xcode, Eclipse, and the command line
([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-08-06-kimi-k3-is-now-available-in-github-copilot/)).
Pricing lands well under the closed frontier models Copilot also offers:
$3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens, with cached
input at $0.30 per million. Independent evaluations have placed Kimi K3's
coding and agentic performance close behind, if not quite at, the very
top closed models, while remaining markedly cheaper to run.

The notable part isn't just that a large lab shipped a strong model — it's
that the model showing up cheapest and open inside the world's most-used
coding assistant isn't from any of the usual closed players. Anyone who
wants to skip Copilot's hosting entirely and run Kimi K3 themselves can
already do so, weights in hand, no API required.
