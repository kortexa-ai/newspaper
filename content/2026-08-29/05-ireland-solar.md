---
title: Ireland's installed solar power crosses 3 gigawatts
kicker: Climate
author: The Editors
summary: A record 1.3 GW peak from utility-scale solar farms last Monday pushed the country's total installed solar capacity past a new milestone, up from a few megawatts a decade ago.
order: 5
---

Ireland's installed solar capacity — rooftop panels and utility-scale
farms combined — passed 3 gigawatts for the first time, the country's
Department of Climate, Energy and the Environment announced Thursday
([Westmeath Examiner](https://www.westmeathexaminer.ie/2026/08/27/solar-generation-in-ireland-reaches-record-levels/)).
The milestone came days after grid-scale solar farms alone hit a record
peak of 1.3 gigawatts on Monday, August 24, briefly supplying more than a
third of the electricity flowing onto the national grid
([Energy Global](https://www.energyglobal.com/solar/28082026/installed-solar-capacity-in-ireland-passes-3-gw-milestone/)).

The scale of the shift is easy to miss year to year: solar capacity on
the Irish grid was measured in single-digit megawatts a decade ago. More
than 200,000 homes now have panels, and a government scheme has fitted
2,000 schools with free solar arrays
([Westmeath Examiner](https://www.westmeathexaminer.ie/2026/08/27/solar-generation-in-ireland-reaches-record-levels/)).
Solar is one piece of a broader build-out — Ireland now has more than 8
gigawatts of renewable generation connected to its grid overall, with
roughly 5 gigawatts of that added in just the past ten years.

"This milestone underlines the growing role solar energy is playing in
Ireland's journey to a low-carbon future," said Nicholas Tarrant,
managing director of ESB Networks, the state-owned operator that connects
the panels to the grid
([Westmeath Examiner](https://www.westmeathexaminer.ie/2026/08/27/solar-generation-in-ireland-reaches-record-levels/)).
It's a modest-sounding number next to the gigawatt totals of larger
countries, but for a grid that had almost no solar on it a decade ago,
crossing 3 gigawatts is the kind of quiet compounding that adds up.
